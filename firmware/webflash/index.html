Install / recover the SB20 Proxy

This flashes the proxy firmware onto your board directly from your browser — no command line. Use it to set up a fresh board or recover one that won't update over the air.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop (Web Serial isn't in Safari/Firefox or on phones).
  2. Plug the board into USB.
  3. Click Install on your board below and pick its serial port.
  4. If the port doesn't appear or flashing won't start: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then retry.

Installing erases the board's settings (WiFi, meter and crank identity).

After it flashes

  1. The board reboots and creates a per-device Wi-Fi network called Setup-XXXX (the OLED/LCD shows the exact name).
  2. Join it from your phone; a setup page opens (or browse to http://172.29.4.1/).
  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi, then your power meter, and save. The board reconnects on your network and shows a live dashboard.

Pairing the bike to it (SB20 crank spoof)

The SB20 only pairs to its own crank, and it looks that crank up by name — so the board has to carry a crank identity the bike is willing to accept.

  1. Find the crank id you want to stand in for — the Stages app lists the bike's stored crank ids (e.g. 51862 : 32059, left then right).
  2. On the board's setup page, open Crank identity (advanced) and put that id in Crank name. Just the number is fine — the Stages  prefix the bike scans for is added for you. Leave it blank to use the board's own unique id (Stages 9NNNN) and type that into the Stages app instead.
  3. Pull the battery of the crank you are standing in for, so two devices never answer to one id.
  4. The bike wants both of its crank ids findable — the one you are spoofing and the real one on the other side. If the other crank is dead too, the bike will refuse to pair.

Crank length is a known quirk: the Stages app won't store it for a spoofed crank, and it does not need to. Once the proxy is in the loop, watts are computed by your pedals — set the crank length in their app (e.g. Favero), not the Stages one.

If ERG mode won't engage

Put the bike in External ride mode in the Stages app. That is the mode where the SB20 takes its target from an outside app over FTMS (Zwift, qz, TrainerRoad). In Grade or Power / Erg the app keeps the target itself, so the bike ignores the workout's targets and your shifters still change resistance.

This is independent of the proxy: the proxy is the bike's power source, while ERG is the bike's own trainer control.
