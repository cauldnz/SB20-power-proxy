Install / recover the SB20 Proxy

This flashes the proxy firmware onto an ESP32-C3 board directly from your browser — no command line. Use it to set up a fresh board or recover one that won't update over the air.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop (Web Serial isn't in Safari/Firefox or on phones).
  2. Plug the board into USB.
  3. Click Install and pick the board's serial port.
  4. If the port doesn't appear or flashing won't start: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then retry.
Your browser can't flash over USB — open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge. This page must be served over HTTPS to flash.

After it flashes

  1. The board reboots and creates a Wi-Fi network called SB20-Setup.
  2. Join it from your phone; a setup page opens (or browse to http://192.168.4.1/).
  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi, then your power meter, and save. The board reconnects on your network and shows a live dashboard.
